How I work

A five-stage process built around research, verification, and realistic design.

  1. Understand

    We begin with the people, not the destination. I learn why you are travelling, where you are considering, your previous Canadian travel, interests, budget, activity level, mobility, transportation preferences, driving tolerance, accommodation preferences, dietary requirements, loyalty programs, schedule, and any requirement for the trip to remain entirely within Canada.

  2. Research

    I investigate possibilities across Canada: destinations, transportation, flights, rail, ferries, driving, cruises, hotels, experiences, accessibility, seasonal considerations, and supplier policies.

  3. Verify

    Canadian travel information can become outdated. Schedules change, seasonal services stop, ferries require reservations, roads close, hotels change policies, cruise itineraries change, and accessibility descriptions may be incomplete. I verify important information against current sources whenever practical.

  4. Design

    The objective is not to include the maximum possible number of places. It is to create a trip with logical routing, reasonable travel times, appropriate pacing, suitable accommodations, and practical transportation, with time to enjoy each destination.

  5. Book and support

    Once the itinerary makes sense, I help coordinate appropriate reservations and remain involved after booking.

Why work with an independent Fora advisor

  • Access to a professional network of preferred hotel, tour, cruise, and on-the-ground partners
  • Support from a community of experienced travel advisors when complex situations need extra perspective
  • Research and design that is independent of any single supplier
  • Itineraries built around you rather than around a pre-packaged trip

I am an independent advisor with Fora, which gives me access to a curated network of travel partners and professional resources. The advice remains my own, but the network means I can draw on broader expertise when it helps your trip.
