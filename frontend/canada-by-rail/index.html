See Canada from the train

Rail as one component of a larger Canadian trip.

Rail travel is one of the most enjoyable ways to see Canada, but it rarely works as a standalone booking. The best rail experiences are planned as part of a larger itinerary that includes hotels before and after the train, transfers, luggage, stopovers, and sometimes flights.

What I research for rail trips

  • Rail routes and schedules
  • Cabin or seat selection
  • Overnight accommodation on and off the train
  • Connections and stopovers
  • Hotels before and after the train segment
  • Accessibility and mobility considerations
  • Luggage policies and transfer logistics
  • Flight connections before and after the rail portion

I do not publish schedules, routes, amenities, or accessibility claims without verifying current information. Details change, and your itinerary should be based on what is actually operating now.
