Go farther north

Remote destinations need more planning, not less.

Northern Canada offers extraordinary landscapes, wildlife, and cultural experiences, but it also brings greater logistical complexity. Limited transportation, seasonal availability, weather, and remote accommodations all require careful advance planning.

What to expect

  • Fewer flights and less frequent transportation
  • Shorter operating seasons
  • Weather that can change plans quickly
  • Remote accommodations with limited inventory
  • Accessibility limitations in some locations
  • Suppliers who need to be contacted directly for reliable details

I never overstate expertise or accessibility for northern destinations. I research and verify individual trips, then present the realistic options.
