British Columbia & Western Canada

From the Pacific coast to the Rocky Mountains.

Based in the Fraser Valley, British Columbia is home ground for Voyages By Dave. But Western Canada is much more than one city or one route. I help travellers understand how Vancouver, Vancouver Island, the Sea-to-Sky corridor, Whistler, the Okanagan, the Canadian Rockies, and Alberta fit into a realistic itinerary.

What makes BC planning complex

  • Ferry logistics. BC Ferries reservations, schedules, and vehicle space can shape an entire Vancouver Island or Sunshine Coast itinerary.
  • Mountain weather and roads. Winter tires, seasonal closures, and avalanches affect timing on mountain highways.
  • Rail and flight connections. Combining the Rocky Mountaineer, VIA Rail, seaplanes, and regional flights requires careful coordination.
  • Accessibility varies by mode. A scenic drive or ferry may not suit every traveller, even when the destination itself is suitable.

Regions I help plan

Vancouver & the Fraser Valley

Urban access, international connections, and the starting point for many BC itineraries.

Vancouver Island & Victoria

Ferries, coastal driving, gardens, wildlife, and accessible options.

Sea-to-Sky & Whistler

Scenic mountain travel, activity choices, and alternatives for non-skiers or slower-paced groups.

Okanagan

Lakes, wine country, and relaxed pacing between mountain and desert landscapes.

Canadian Rockies

Banff and Jasper access from BC, plus scenic drives and rail options.

Alberta

Calgary, Edmonton, and routes connecting the prairies to the mountains.

Rather than listing attractions, my value is in figuring out how the places should fit together for your group, your timing, and your mobility needs.
