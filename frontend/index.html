Need the entire trip to stay in Canada?

For some travellers, staying within Canada is more than a preference. Insurance coverage, mobility, documentation, health considerations, personal preference, or other circumstances may make an international border crossing impractical.

I can research itineraries specifically around that requirement, checking more than the destination name: flight routings, cruise ports, ferry routes, rail routes, ground transportation, excursions, and connections.

Accessible & multigenerational travel

An “accessible” label rarely tells the whole story. I research the details that matter: step-free access, walk distances, accessible transportation, washroom usability, terrain, seating, and equipment rental.

Multigenerational trips need thoughtful pacing so parents, children, and grandparents can all enjoy the journey without exhaustion.

How I work

  1. Understand. I begin with the people, not the destination. Why you're travelling, who is coming, what you need, and any requirement for the trip to stay in Canada.
  2. Research. I investigate destinations, transportation, flights, rail, ferries, cruises, hotels, experiences, accessibility, and seasonal considerations.
  3. Verify. Canadian travel information changes. I cross-check schedules, ferry reservations, road status, cruise itineraries, and accessibility claims with current sources whenever practical.
  4. Design. I turn the research into a realistic itinerary with logical routing, reasonable travel times, appropriate pacing, and practical transportation.
  5. Book and support. Once the itinerary makes sense, I help coordinate appropriate reservations and stay involved after booking.

Canada is too big for guesswork

Planning travel across Canada can involve dozens of sources: airlines, rail operators, ferries, cruise lines, hotels, tourism organizations, parks, attractions, transportation providers, and accessibility information.

I use research technology I've developed to help search, organize and cross-check information from relevant sources. The technology helps me investigate more possibilities and find details worth verifying. It does not decide where you should go—I do.

Technology helps with the research. Evidence helps establish the facts. Experience and judgment turn those facts into a trip.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a travel advisor for a trip inside Canada?

Canada feels familiar because it's home, but it's also enormous. Vancouver and Halifax are thousands of kilometres apart, ferries affect itineraries in British Columbia and Atlantic Canada, rail works very differently from flying, and remote destinations may have limited transportation. You can absolutely plan it yourself; I help when you don't want to figure out every detail alone.

Can you keep the entire trip inside Canada?

Yes, when that matters. I research each segment—flights, cruise ports, ferry routes, rail, ground transportation, and excursions—to confirm whether the complete trip actually remains within Canada. You remain responsible for confirming insurance coverage and any entry or document requirements with the relevant provider or authority.

Do you book international travel?

Canada is my primary focus. I also help existing clients and travellers with selected international cruises, diving, expedition, and complex travel.
