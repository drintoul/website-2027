Frequently asked questions

What makes Voyages By Dave different from a regular travel agency?

Canada is the primary area of expertise, not an afterthought. I focus on research, realistic itinerary design, and verification. My product is a thoughtfully designed trip, not just a flight, hotel, or cruise booking.

Do I need a travel advisor for a trip inside Canada?

Canada feels familiar because it's home, but it's also enormous. Vancouver and Halifax are thousands of kilometres apart, ferries affect itineraries in British Columbia and Atlantic Canada, rail works very differently from flying, and remote destinations may have limited transportation. You can absolutely plan it yourself; I help when you don't want to figure out every detail alone.

Can you keep the entire trip inside Canada?

Yes, when that matters. I research each segment to confirm whether the complete trip actually remains within Canada. You remain responsible for confirming insurance coverage and any entry or document requirements with the relevant provider or authority.

How does the planning fee work?

The $200 planning deposit covers the research and itinerary design completed before reservations are made and confirms that we're both committed to moving forward.

Do you book international travel?

Canada is my primary focus. I also help existing clients and travellers with selected international cruises, diving, expedition, and complex travel.

Do you use AI?

I use research-assisted technology that I have developed to search, organize, and cross-check information. The technology helps me investigate more possibilities; I review and verify the results. The final decisions are mine.

What if I have already done my own research?

Perfect. Send me what you have. I'll help identify what looks good, what deserves another look, and what may be missing.

What if I prefer booking with Costco or another large retailer?

That can be a good choice. Costco Travel and similar services are excellent for straightforward, price-sensitive trips where you already know what you want and don't need custom research or itinerary design. If your main goal is the lowest price on a simple booking, they may be the better fit. My service is designed for trips that need research, verification, and design before anything is booked. If that doesn't describe your trip, there is no reason to pay a planning deposit.

What are the advantages of working with a Fora travel advisor?

As an independent Fora advisor, I combine my own research and decision-making with Fora's professional travel advisor network and curated partner relationships. That means you get personal, independent advice backed by a community of experienced advisors, access to preferred hotel, tour, cruise, and on-the-ground partners, and extra support when a complex situation needs more than one perspective.
